Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on identity lookups, virtual accounts, and developer tools.

How does wallet funding work on SmartLink NG?

Every registered user and agent receives automated dedicated virtual bank account numbers (Wema Bank, Moniepoint, Sterling, Providus). Any bank transfer made to your virtual account is credited to your wallet in real time with zero manual delay.

How do developers integrate SmartLink's identity verification & VTU APIs?

Developers can generate live and sandbox API keys directly from their dashboard. We offer high-performance RESTful JSON endpoints with sub-450ms response latency, complete Postman collections, and automated webhooks.

What are the requirements for CAC Business Name & Company Registration?

To register a Business Name or Company with CAC, you need: 1) Two proposed business names, 2) Nature of business, 3) Valid government ID, 4) Passport photo, and 5) Official business address. Filings are processed in 48 to 72 hours.

How do I verify and download my NIN Slip in Nigeria?

Verify your National Identity Number (NIN) instantly by providing your 11-digit NIN or registered phone number. The portal retrieves verified records and generates standard or premium PDF slips with tamper-evident QR codes.
